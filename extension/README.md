# DealOS Job Manager — Browser Extension (Manifest V3)

A high-performance Chrome/Edge browser extension that **autofills job application forms in one click** across all major Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, LinkedIn Easy Apply, SmartRecruiters, BambooHR, and corporate career portals using your verified DealOS career facts.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **⚡ 1-Click Form Autofill:** Automatically detects and fills First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio, City, Current Employer, Current Title, Years of Experience, Notice Period, and Compensation.
- **🎯 Smart ATS Detection:** Real-time badge indicating whether you're on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, or a standard corporate job form.
- **📥 1-Click Save to DealOS Tracker:** Scrapes the job title, employer, description, and link from the current page and adds it straight into your DealOS application pipeline.
- **📋 Click-to-Copy Chips:** Quickly copy Email, Phone, LinkedIn URL, or summary pitch to clipboard.
- **🔄 Cloud Profile Sync:** Seamlessly syncs with your authenticated profile on `https://jobs.dealos.in` or local workspace.

---

## 🛠️ Installation in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

1. Open your browser and navigate to the Extensions page:
   - **Chrome:** `chrome://extensions`
   - **Edge:** `edge://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button.
4. Select the `Job-Manager\Job-Manager\extension` directory in this project.
5. The **DealOS Job Manager** extension is now installed! Pin it to your extension toolbar for quick access.

---

## 🧪 Testing on an Application Page

1. Open any job application page (e.g., an Ashby, Lever, or Greenhouse job posting).
2. Click the DealOS extension icon in your browser toolbar.
3. Click **"Auto-Fill Application"**.
4. The extension will automatically populate the inputs and highlight them in emerald green!
